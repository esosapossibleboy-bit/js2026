Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Discover how to make the ultimate soft , chewy, and perfectly golden chocolate chip cookies from scratch with this easy recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup granulated white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Cream together the softened butter and granulated sugar until the mixture is smooth.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, making sure they are fully incorporated.
  4. Stir in the chocolate chips, drop spoonfuls of dough onto the baking sheet, and bake for 10 minutes.
